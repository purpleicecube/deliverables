1. Regulatory Landscape

  • Data‑Privacy (GDPR, CCPA, CPRA): Penalties up to 4 % of global revenue; requires consent tracking, breach notification within 72 hours, and cross‑border transfer controls.
  • Wage‑and‑Hour (FLSA, DOL updates): Misclassification and overtime errors trigger $500 K–$2 M in back‑pay and fines per incident.
  • VMS/MSP Compliance: Real‑time reporting of bill‑rate markup, worker classification, and placement metrics is contract‑mandated; violations risk termination and revenue loss.
  • Security Standards (ISO 27001, SOC 2): Ongoing controls are mandatory for enterprise clients; non‑compliance harms reputation and can lead to loss of certification.
  • Industry Insight: 23 % of staffing firms experience at least one breach annually, with an average remediation cost of $1.1 M.
These overlapping obligations create a compliance matrix that is costly to maintain manually, driving the need for an integrated, intelligent solution.
